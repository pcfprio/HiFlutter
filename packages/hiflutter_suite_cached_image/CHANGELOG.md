## 0.1.0

- Initial release: `HiCachedImage` and `HiImageViewer` backed by
  `cached_network_image`, with empty-URL short-circuiting, compact Material 3
  loading/error defaults, `HiError` normalization, `imageProviderOverride` /
  `imageProviderBuilder` test hooks, and pinch-to-zoom gallery navigation
  without exposing third-party types.
