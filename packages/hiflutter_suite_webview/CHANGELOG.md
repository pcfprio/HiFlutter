## 0.1.0

- Initial release: `HiWebViewPage`, a `webview_flutter`-backed Material 3 WebView
  page with progress bar (`0`–`99`), automatic title tracking, `PopScope` and
  back-button history navigation, `allowedHosts` / non-HTTP(S) handoff via
  `HiLinkOpener`, and `HiErrorView` recovery without leaking third-party types.
