## 0.1.0

- Initial release: `HiSuiteLocalizations` (with `gen-l10n` English/Chinese
  defaults, delegate/parameter overrides, and `HiError` display mapping),
  `HiSuiteMetrics`, state views (`HiLoadingView`, `HiEmptyView`, `HiErrorView`,
  `HiStateView`), `HiPaginationController` with `HiPageSlice`,
  `HiPaginatedListView` with `HiLoadMoreFooter`, `HiToast` / `HiToastHandle`,
  `HiConfirmDialog`, `HiListSection`, `HiListRow`, `HiSelectableListRow`,
  `HiPrimaryButton` / `HiButtonShape`, and `HiSearchField`.
